## More to account for

Battery monitoring already uses IOKit change notifications ([BatteryActivityManager.swift:90](../boringNotch/managers/BatteryActivityManager.swift#L90)). Its one-second sleep spaces queued notifications; it is not a permanent one-second battery poll. Keep this distinction when searching for timers.

Audio capture is shared and deduplicates unchanged PIDs ([AudioCaptureManager.swift:315](../boringNotch/managers/AudioCaptureManager.swift#L315)). Multiple windows do not establish duplicate captures. Spotify and Apple Music use notifications; YouTube stops fallback polling when WebSocket delivery activates. Random visualizer animation already requests reduced frame rates. A repeating animation declaration alone does not prove hidden GPU work.

Camera has explicit sleep teardown and guarded wake recovery ([CameraSessionEngine.swift:494](../boringNotch/managers/CameraSessionEngine.swift#L494)). Reuse existing lifecycle owners rather than inventing a general power framework. System sleep, display sleep, screen lock, fullscreen hiding and no visible consumer are different states. Low Power Mode is already observed for battery presentation, but cosmetic capture does not consume that signal. Any automatic visual degradation would be a proposed product choice, not an accepted policy.

The planning vault remains canonical. Efficiency work must preserve the one-way Today display contract, truthful bridge state, focus persistence, reminder delivery and bounded source actions. No vault scanning, payload mutation or broad settings mode belongs in this pass.
